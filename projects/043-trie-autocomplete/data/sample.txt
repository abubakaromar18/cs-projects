The theory of computation asks what can be computed and how efficiently. A trie
is a tree in which every path from the root spells a prefix, so the time to find
a prefix depends on the length of the prefix and not on the number of stored
words. That is the reason tries are the classic structure behind autocomplete,
spell checkers, and routing tables. The trie stores each word once; the
frequency of the word tells the ranking how popular it is. The more often a
word is typed, the higher it ranks. Think of the search box in a browser: you
type "th" and it offers the, that, this, then, there, they, think, through.
Those suggestions are ranked by how frequently each word was seen. Training the
trie on a corpus of text gives realistic frequencies. Tree, trees, tries,
trial, train, training, transfer, transform, translate, transport, travel.
The prefix "tr" is shared by all of them, so they share the same branch in the
tree. Throughput, thread, threads, threshold, throw, thrown, thumb, thunder.
Programming, program, programs, programmer, progress, project, projects,
prefix, prefixes, prefer, preference, prepare, present, preserve, pressure.
